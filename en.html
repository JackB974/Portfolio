How I work with AI

Ongoing reflection · work in progress…

I see AI as an extraordinary technology, particularly because it helps me turn ideas into projects more quickly. I often have more ideas than time to explore them.

I take the role of project lead and creator of ideas. AI and agents form my working team: I define the need, choose the stack, set the constraints and make the decisions; they help me explore options, compare approaches and implement.

  • Design: define the idea, scope and architecture of the project.
  • Review: examine the generated code, test it and understand the choices behind it.
  • Learn: use each project to improve and remain able to work on the code without assistance.
  • Experiment: move several projects forward in parallel while remaining accountable for the result.
